# ROM checklist (for testing)

Commercial games are bring-your-own-ROM: use copies you own (dumped from your own cartridges or discs). The site never uploads them; they stay in the browser.

On a game's page, press **LOAD YOUR ROM** (or **Start game → Load your copy**) and pick the file below.
